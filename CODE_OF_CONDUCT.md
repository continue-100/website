# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Prismio to be a friendly, respectful, and harassment-free experience
for everyone, whatever their background or level of experience. Technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Our standard

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That document is part of this Code of Conduct and applies in full to every space the project
runs: this repository, the compiler repository, issues and pull requests, the Discord server,
and any other place where someone represents the project.

In short:

**Expected**

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or sexualized attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainer at **saksham6975@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately, and the identity of the person reporting is kept confidential.

Maintainers who are involved in a report will not take part in deciding how to handle it.
